# Your answer

Return JSON and nothing else, with exactly these three fields:

```json
{{
  "answer": "",
  "confirmed": null,
  "targets": []
}}
```

The shape above is the empty form, not an answer. Fill it in from the image in
front of you.

- `answer` - what you actually see, in one or two sentences.
- `confirmed` - stays null unless the question was a yes/no check about the
  state of the screen; then it is true or false, and `answer` says what you
  based it on.
- `targets` - one entry per thing on the screen you are pointing at. Each is an
  object with exactly four fields: `label`, what the thing is called; `x` and
  `y`, whole numbers of pixels in the frame described above; and `confidence`,
  between 0 and 1. An empty list is a correct answer.

# Rules

- Describe what is on the screen, not what you expect a screen like this to
  show. If the page is still loading, blank, or shows an error, say so.
- A target is something you can actually see. Put the coordinate at the centre
  of the thing, not at its edge or its label.
- Use a low `confidence` when you are guessing at a position, and say in
  `answer` that you are.
- Give no targets rather than invented ones. A wrong coordinate makes the
  machine click on something else, which is worse than not clicking.
